## Detailed description

Not sure which Business Analysis path fits you? Answer two or three plain-language questions about your experience and where you want to go, and get a personalised career pathway. No certification jargon needed.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tell us how much hands-on Business Analysis experience you have (counting work even if your job title was never "Business Analyst").
2. Choose your next career goal: certification, Agile or product ownership, data analytics, technical BA, AI, or a specific industry.
3. Get your recommended path.

WHAT YOU GET
• A step-by-step learning roadmap for your goal
• A recommended certification and matching courses, labelled primary, also recommended or optional
• A plain-language explanation of why, plus an eligibility and scope note so you know what to confirm before applying
• Links to the official credential issuer where the credential is not issued by IIBA

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs on your device. No account, no tracking, no data collection, no browser permissions.

## Firefox notes for reviewers
The extension is built with WXT + Vite + React. To reproduce the build from the attached source zip:
`pnpm install && pnpm --filter browser-extension zip:firefox` (output: `.output/browser-extension-1.0.0-firefox.zip`) (Node 22.9+, pnpm 9+).
The two `UNSAFE_VAR_ASSIGNMENT` lint warnings are inside the bundled React DOM library, not extension code.
