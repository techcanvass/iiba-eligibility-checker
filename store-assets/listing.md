## Detailed description

Not sure which Business Analyst certification fits you? Answer a few plain-language questions about your background and the work you actually do, and get a personalised shortlist. No certification jargon needed.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tell us about yourself: experience, background, education and what interests you.
2. Tick the everyday activities you've done (interviewing users, writing user stories, building business cases…), even if your job title was never "Business Analyst".
3. See which IIBA and PMI business analysis certifications match your experience.

WHAT YOU GET
• A status for each certification: no prerequisite, potential match, or what still needs verifying
• Experience counted only within each certification's time window (7 years for CCBA, 10 for CBAP, 8 for PMI-PBA)
• An optional precise CCBA/CBAP check using your exact hours per knowledge area
• The PD hours, references and contact hours you'll need at application time
• Links to the official IIBA and PMI certification pages

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs on your device. No account, no tracking, no data collection, no browser permissions.

## Firefox notes for reviewers
The extension is built with WXT + Vite + React. To reproduce the build from the attached source zip:
`pnpm install && pnpm --filter browser-extension zip:firefox` (output: `.output/browser-extension-1.0.0-firefox.zip`) (Node 22.9+, pnpm 9+).
The two `UNSAFE_VAR_ASSIGNMENT` lint warnings are inside the bundled React DOM library, not extension code.
